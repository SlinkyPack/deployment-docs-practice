# Site Checklist

Work through this list on site, in order. Tick each item before moving on.

## Before arriving

- Site survey report printed and signed
- Correct unit type confirmed against the work order
- Spare fuses in the toolkit
- Customer contact name and phone number

## On arrival

- Mounting surface clear, dry and undamaged
- Cable route matches the survey drawing
- No live conductors exposed at the supply point
- Working area cordoned off if required

## Pre-power checks
- Fuse fitted and correctly rated
- Polarity confirmed at the terminal block
- Earth stud torqued to 1.2 Nm
- Sensor cable routed away from the power cable

## After power-up

- Registration completed within two minutes
- Self-test shows a steady green indicator
- No fault codes logged
- Commissioning report completed and signed

## Before leaving

- Enclosure closed and secured
- Tools and packaging removed from site
- Customer walked through the front panel indicators
- Report handed over
